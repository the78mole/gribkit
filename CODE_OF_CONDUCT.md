# Code of Conduct

gribkit is a public project with one maintainer. This code of conduct applies to all project
spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commit messages and any communication in
which someone represents the project.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part without harassment, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be friendly and respectful, also when you disagree.
- Criticise code, data and methods objectively, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback, admit mistakes and learn from them.
- Credit sources, licenses and the work of others correctly.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Public or private harassment, including repeatedly contacting someone against their wishes.
- Publishing other people's private information (such as a postal or email address) without
  their explicit permission.
- Knowingly adding third-party material without the rights to it.
- Other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what violates this code of conduct and may edit or remove comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not comply with it. Depending on the
severity, the consequence is a note, a warning, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
Decisions are usually explained to the person concerned.

## Reporting

Please report incidents confidentially to the maintainer, GitHub user
[the78mole](https://github.com/the78mole) (contact details on the profile). Every report is
reviewed, treated confidentially and answered. If you disagree with a decision, you can appeal
the same way.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.
